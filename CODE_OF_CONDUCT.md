# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent. Assume the person you are replying to is trying to build something
good, and that they know things you do not.

## What that means here

- Criticise the change, not the person who wrote it. "This drops the
  append-only guarantee" is useful; "this is careless" is not.
- Explain a rejection. Somebody spent their evening on it.
- English or 中文, whichever you are comfortable in. Neither is anybody's
  fault.

## What is not welcome

Harassment, personal attacks, and unwelcome attention of any kind. Publishing
someone's private information. Deliberately derailing a discussion.

## Enforcement

Report it privately to the maintainers. Reports stay confidential. Depending
on what happened, the response ranges from a private word to a permanent ban;
the maintainers decide, and will say what they decided and why.

This is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1, kept short on purpose.
